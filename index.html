About Us

ZY Hardware is a professional manufacturer specializing in cabinet hinges and drawer slides. With strong capabilities in product development, tooling, machinery integration and manufacturing, we have become a trusted OEM supplier for many well-known international brands.

With over 15 years of industry experience, advanced production facilities and strict quality control systems, we offer a wide range of standard and customized furniture hardware solutions.

Our product portfolio includes:

  • Soft Close Undermount Drawer Slides
  • Soft Close Ball Bearing Slides
  • Push Open Drawer Slides
  • Soft Close Cabinet Hinges
  • Electric Double Wall Drawer Systems
  • Lift Systems & Intelligent Hardware
  • Customized OEM & ODM Solutions

Our products are widely used in furniture, kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, industrial machinery and automotive applications worldwide.
